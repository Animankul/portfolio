The thinking
behind the engineer

I enjoy working on problems where software needs to understand information, make decisions and execute multi-step workflows.

That's what draws me toward Agentic AI. The interesting challenge isn't simply generating an answer — it's building a reliable system that can observe, reason, act and produce useful results.

Every taal (rhythm cycle) has constraints, just like every system has requirements — the interesting part happens in the space between them. Bols like Dha, Tin, Na, Ge build complex patterns from simple rules, the same way agents compose simple steps into something intelligent.

My approach combines software engineering, AI experimentation and continuous testing. I enjoy taking ideas from prototypes toward practical systems that solve real problems.
