05 — Beyond Code

The rhythm
behind the engineer

When I'm not architecting APIs or training neural networks, I'm at the tabla — India's classical percussion instrument. The tabla teaches you something that no textbook can: the discipline of structure within improvisation.

Every taal (rhythm cycle) has constraints, just like every system has requirements. The magic — whether in music or in software — happens in the space between those constraints.

Bols like Dha, Tin, Na, Ge combine into patterns of extraordinary complexity from simple rules. Sound familiar? That's algorithmic thinking — just set to 16-beat cycles instead of clock cycles.
